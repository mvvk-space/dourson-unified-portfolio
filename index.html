Dan's Focus: Terrestrial Organisms

Dan is a wildlife biologist who has an interest in all terrestrial organisms. For the past 30 years, his focus has been the study of land snails. He has studied land snails in Kentucky, West Virginia, and in the Great Smoky Mountains National Park in the USA.

In Central America, his research has focused on Belize, Guatemala, a cursory survey in the Tamishyacu-Tahuayo Reserve in Peru, and currently, Panama with STRI.
